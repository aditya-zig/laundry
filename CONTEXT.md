# Laundry

Laundry coordinates a customer's garment-care request across delivery and operations. This glossary uses the selected 18 October product direction; business rules and implementation details belong in the plan.

## Language

**Customer**:
The person requesting laundry service and receiving the cared-for garments.

**Delivery partner**:
The person responsible for collecting and returning the customer's laundry.
_Avoid_: Partner when it could mean a business cofounder or a laundry shop.

**Operations**:
The role coordinating an order's processing and delivery handoffs. Which people hold this role remains a product decision.

**Estimated weight**:
The quantity of laundry anticipated before it is physically measured.
_Avoid_: Final weight.

**Measured weight**:
The quantity determined by physically weighing the laundry.
_Avoid_: Estimated weight when referring to an intake measurement.

**Box**:
A transport container allocated to carry laundry through collection and return.
_Avoid_: Weight or garment count when referring to the number of containers.
