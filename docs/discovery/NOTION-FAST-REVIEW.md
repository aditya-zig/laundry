## Recorded questions and answers

## Product and launch

### Q01 · What should this planning session cover?
**Answer:** Whole product: customer app, rider app and operations.
**Options:** Customer booking journey; visuals only; whole product.

### Q07 · What counts as the first release being ready?
**Answer:** Controlled live pilot, preceded by a connected demo.
**Options:** A: connected demo; B: controlled live pilot; C: broad launch.

### Q08 · Which platforms are required initially?
**Answer:** Android customer/rider apps and operations web. iPhone later.
**Options:** Android + ops web; mobile web/PWA first; Android + iPhone at launch.

### Q09 · Which services belong in the initial catalog?
**Answer:** Wash-and-fold and wash-and-iron initially.
**Options:** Open catalog question. Recommended: wash-and-fold + wash-and-iron. Other candidates: dry cleaning, steam press.

### Q10 · What should Laundry borrow from the Zepto reference?
**Answer:** Familiar shopping/navigation and quick selection, with Laundry’s own identity.
**Options:** A: shopping/navigation; B: compact density; C: wider visual redesign.

### Q12 · What should launch when Premium is undefined?
**Answer:** Premium should offer more care and speed; Q15 refines the promise.
**Options:** General first; faster Premium; distinct-care Premium.

### Q15 · What concrete Premium promise should the app show?
**Answer:** Extra care + priority handling. Exact inclusions, price and supported speed promise are still open.
**Options:** Extra care + priority, promise later; guaranteed faster return; Premium coming soon.

## People and service operation

### Q11 · Who will operate the console and record weight/progress/problems?
**Answer:** Pay the delivery person to help operate the service. Permissions are narrowed in Q11.a and Q23.
**Options:** Founder/cofounder; separate employee; laundry staff. Founder nominated the paid rider.

### Q11.a · Which responsibilities should the paid delivery person have?
**Answer:** Rider handles pickup/return records; founders control pricing, refunds and final processing. Q23 later adds weighing/inspection.
**Options:** 1: field work, founders control decisions; 2: add intake/progress; 3: full console including money.

### Q14 · What is the first live service area?
**Answer:** Indiranagar. Exact service boundary still to be supplied.
**Options:** Beniganalli; Indiranagar; another explicit boundary.

### Q17 · What morning/evening hours and closed days should be planned?
**Answer:** Tentative: 8 pm → next-day 8 am; fallback 5 pm → next-day 8 am; weekends 10 am → 5 pm. Feasibility and exact windows are open.
**Options:** Open-ended hours/closed-days question; no fixed multiple-choice menu.

### Q19 · How should overnight and weekend timing be offered?
**Answer:** Offer overnight and weekend same-day options only when capacity permits.
**Options:** Prove pilot target; guarantee at launch; capacity-dependent overnight/weekend options.

### Q23 · Who physically weighs and inspects at the laundry?
**Answer:** The paid rider weighs and inspects at the laundry.
**Options:** Laundry operator; paid rider at laundry; founder.

### Q24 · What follows unsuccessful increased-price approval contact?
**Answer:** A founder agrees an unprocessed return if approval remains unanswered. Timeout, attempts and fees are deferred.
**Options:** Founder agrees return, details later; hold indefinitely; defer entire policy.

### Q34 · Who controls whether slots can accept more orders?
**Answer:** Founder controls slot capacity/opening with rider/operator input; full slots stop accepting bookings.
**Options:** Founder with rider/operator input; rider; laundry operator.

## Quantity, payment and exceptions

### Q13 · If a 5 kg estimate measures 7 kg, what happens?
**Answer:** Show an estimate; obtain customer approval for increased measured-weight pricing before processing.
**Options:** Measured price with approval; absorb difference; fixed box price.

### Q16 · When should the customer pay?
**Answer:** Pay at delivery/return. Price-increase approval still happens before processing.
**Options:** After intake before processing; at return; adjusted prepayment.

### Q18 · How should boxes and customer quantity selection work?
**Answer:** Per-kg pricing with an approximate size guide, e.g. 1 kg ≈ 5–6 shirts. Physical box allocation is still open.
**Options:** Operations assigns boxes; automatic kg-to-box mapping; customer chooses boxes. Founder redirected to a kg size guide.

### Q20 · What are rates, Premium pricing, minimums and billing increments?
**Answer:** DEFERRED: agree rates, Premium surcharge, minimums and increments together. Discuss this first.
**Options:** Provide rates/minimums/increments, or explicitly defer undecided numbers.

### Q21 · What if increased-weight approval receives no answer?
**Answer:** Hold processing and contact the customer; no automatic increase or processing.
**Options:** Hold/contact; timed unprocessed return; pre-agreed increase allowance.

### Q22 · What payment methods should the rider support at return?
**Answer:** UPI and cash at return.
**Options:** UPI + cash; UPI only; payment link + fallback.

### Q26 · If the customer estimates 5 kg but intake measures 4 kg, how should billing work?
**Answer:** Proposed charging the selected estimate even when measured weight is lower. Q29 defers the final minimum-package rule.
**Options:** Charge lower measured weight; keep original estimate; discuss with partner.

### Q27 · When may a customer cancel?
**Answer:** Free cancellation before pickup; a founder handles requests after pickup. Later charges/rules remain open.
**Options:** Free before pickup, founder after; founder reviews all; defer policy.

### Q28 · What happens if UPI fails or the customer cannot pay at return?
**Answer:** Try an agreed alternative, then contact a founder. Keep payment unresolved; do not falsely mark payment/delivery complete.
**Options:** Alternative then founder; approved pay-later limit; defer handling.

### Q29 · Should the selected kg be a clearly disclosed minimum package quantity?
**Answer:** DEFERRED: discuss the minimum-package/lower-weight billing rule with the partner. Q26 is not final policy.
**Options:** Disclosed minimum package; revise to lower measured billing; discuss with partner.

### Q30 · What should happen if clothes are missing or damaged?
**Answer:** DEFERRED: decide the entire missing/damaged-clothes policy together.
**Options:** Founder investigates, limits later; rider settles within limit; discuss full policy.
