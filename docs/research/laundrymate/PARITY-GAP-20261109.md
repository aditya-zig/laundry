## 4. Risks

- Notify-only subscription pages being counted as parity. #42 states "Disabled or notify-me is not delivered subscription parity" and PLAN L87 repeats "'notify me' is not subscription parity", so a shipped plans-plus-notify page plus a disabled slice cannot close #42 without the partner rules first.
- Synthetic evidence being narrated as production capability. PLAN L12 names demo identities, simulated capture and the messaging outbox explicitly test-only, CI L96-L97 forbids relabeling manual collection as gateway capture, and CI L110-L112 says the checkpoint is local with no deployment, live ordering, real payment or provider activation authorized. The failure mode lives in reporting as much as in code.
- Parity pressure producing invented fulfillment promises. PLAN L80 bars fake GPS and invented delivery promises, and #40 says stage count follows honest domain events rather than an imposed six-stage fiction. Time pressure on the client side is the worked example: CI L61-L63 shows how a real defect (stale bag quantity) hides under optimistic assertions until a browser run catches it.
- The human gate row stays empty. #45's checkboxes are all unchecked at fetch on 2026-11-09, and customer-support L45 records the tracker re-read with zero comments and no recorded approvals, so engineering must continue without inventing rates, capacity or identities (PLAN L11). Three parity slices are directly downstream of this sheet.
- A committed-revision checkpoint was open on recent slices at read time. BILL L19 marked its billing journey receipt dirty and STAFF L21-L22 left the clean-revision four-role journey plus recording and restore archive open; the receipts recorded at e41049b95c801f087343b58db61005d1172a93e6 close both locally (clean-revision-checkpoint-20261109.md). Those stay local receipts, not GitHub CI results (CI L110), so a push-triggered workflow run remains the stronger evidence local runs cannot substitute for.

## 5. Proposed execution order

The founder's own plan already sequences these, and PLAN L13-L15 says dates move openly rather than gates getting waived. This order respects two constraints from the task: never reopen live ordering and keep every parity slice on task branches with pushed SHAs.

1. First, land clean-revision receipts for the two scopes whose current runs are dirty: the billing ledger card (BILL L19) and the support console integration (STAFF L20-L21). These are small, and IMPL L166 says any changed source SHA invalidates prior passing evidence, so re-pinning them on committed SHAs keeps sections 1 to 4 of this report citable without an asterisk. Done locally the same day at e41049b95c801f087343b58db61005d1172a93e6 (clean-revision-checkpoint-20261109.md); the remaining part is the same evidence from a push-triggered CI run.
2. Verify the Day-5 family (#37, #38, #39, #40) together, against gate P5 at PLAN L81. #37's honest empty, unavailable and error states can run on owned API config before #45 supplies partner rates; they need no invented configuration. #38 needs policy wording from #45 for anything that sounds like a promise (turnaround, refunds, claims), so the copy stays silent where no policy is approved rather than borrowing competitor promises (REF L111, L165 and L212). #39 is the accessible responsive walkthrough plus the API regression on one SHA. #40 keeps stage count tied to real domain events. No slice in this pass touches ordering enablement.
3. Then #43 rider and ops speed, assigned to Day 6 in PLAN L85. It is the engineering slice least dependent on #45, since the issue body scopes it against the existing acceptance set.
4. Pursue #45 continuously in parallel from step 2; PLAN L97 charges the founder deliverables to #28. Checkbox by checkbox this means: area and facility with rates, taxes, minimums and rounding; turnaround, cutoffs, calendars and capacity; intake, cancellation, care, claim, refund and support policies; the identity channel and staff MFA; credentials and compliance; the spending allowance; named owners and real phone availability; rehearsal booking.
5. #41 and #42 only after #45 supplies their inputs: #41 partner rates and calendars, #42 lifecycle rules plus the founder spending decision. Both bodies accept the feature staying disabled and blocked until then; #42 already rejects "notify me" as delivered parity.
6. Save #44 for last: integrated regression, operational rehearsal and feature-matrix review on the final SHA, with each slice reported as delivered, partial, blocked or later honestly (PLAN L89). The five consented physical orders rehearse the changed behavior and sit behind #45's booking checkbox.
7. Across all steps, close nothing from a diagnostic or single-role run: #29's body closes slices only on a current tested SHA with real API/PostgreSQL/browser or native evidence, and diagnostic runs are deliberately counted separately (CI L53-L54 kept an uncommitted diagnostic pass distinct from clean-revision acceptance).
